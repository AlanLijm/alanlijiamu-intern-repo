# Internship Time Plan — Focus Bear (Monash Generator Program)

**Total required hours:** 180 hours
**Duration:** 12 weeks
**Working days:** Tuesday & Thursday (2 days/week)
**Hours per session:** 7.5 hours
**Weekly total:** 15 hours (2 × 7.5h)
**Location:** Hybrid — home (remote) or office, using GitHub + Focus Bear app for the onboarding repo and later the Focus Bear product codebase
**Time tracking:** All sessions logged via the **Focus Bear app** (Focus Session started at the beginning of each work block, stopped at the end), used as evidence of hours worked

---

## Weekly Schedule Template

| Day | Time | Duration | Location | Activity |
|-----|------|----------|----------|----------|
| Tuesday | 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (incl. 1h lunch break) | 7.5h | Hybrid (home or office) | Internship work, tracked via Focus Bear |
| Thursday | 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (incl. 1h lunch break) | 7.5h | Hybrid (home or office) | Internship work, tracked via Focus Bear |

---

## Phase 1 — Onboarding (Weeks 1–4)

Goal: Complete all 79 onboarding issues in the duplicated Focus Bear repo, working milestone by milestone (Milestone 0 → Milestone 10, lowest number first, increasing complexity), ~2–3 milestones/week.

| Week | Tue | Thu | Milestones covered | Target issues closed (cumulative) |
|------|-----|-----|---------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | Repo setup + Milestone 0 issues | Milestone 1–2 issues | Milestone 0 – 2 | ~20 / 79 |
| 2 | Milestone 3 issues | Milestone 4–5 issues | Milestone 3 – 5 | ~40 / 79 |
| 3 | Milestone 6 issues | Milestone 7–8 issues | Milestone 6 – 8 | ~60 / 79 |
| 4 | Milestone 9 issues, wrap up remaining | Milestone 10 issues, close/tick all checkboxes, notify team leader for review | Milestone 9 – 10 | 79 / 79 |

## Weeks 5–12 — Remaining Internship Hours

Same weekly schedule (Tuesday & Thursday, 7.5h/session, hybrid) continues for the remaining 8 weeks, covering whatever tasks the team leader assigns after the onboarding review (e.g. Focus Bear app feature/bug work). Content will be filled in once assigned.

---

## Hour Tracking Summary

- 12 weeks × 2 sessions/week × 7.5 hours/session = **180 hours**
- Every session is started/stopped as a Focus Session in the Focus Bear app, providing a time-stamped log as evidence of hours completed
- If a Tuesday/Thursday session is missed (e.g. due to part-time work conflicts), it will be made up later the same week to keep the 180-hour total on track

---

## Notes

- Exact task content in Phase 2 (Weeks 5–12) will be adjusted based on the team leader's actual assignments after the onboarding review.
- This plan assumes the internship starts in Week 1 of this cycle; dates will be filled in against the actual calendar once confirmed with the team leader.
