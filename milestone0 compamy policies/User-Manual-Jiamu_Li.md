# 0.5 My role

I am working on backend development for Focus Bear as part of my internship.
The days and times I work are: Tuesdays and Thursdays, approximately 7.5 hours per session, hybrid (home or office).
How I Work Best
I do my best work when I have clear, direct instructions and enough time to process ideas before responding.
I struggle when tasks are messy or unstructured — it makes me feel anxious and slows me down.
Communication Style
I prefer messages to be direct and detailed. Written communication works best for me, since I can plan my task and adjust my priorities based on what's written.
The best way to reach me is through a written message rather than a call.
If you need something urgent, do this: send me a clear, written message — I'll adjust my priorities accordingly once I see it.
Collaboration & Teamwork
I work best in a team when there's a clear goal and someone leading or guiding the group.
My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is too much unstructured freedom — without enough direction, it's easy to hit obstacles and run into various problems.
If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I'm listening to and absorbing other people's opinions and ideas, learning from their thinking.
Focus & Productivity
I stay focused by listening to music — it calms me down and helps me feel more productive — and by working in a quiet environment. A structured to-do list also helps me identify which tasks are essential so I can adjust my priorities.
Things that distract me: having too many messy, disorganized tasks at once, which makes me feel anxious.
The best way to support my productivity is to give me clear instructions and written feedback, along with a quiet environment and some music.
Feedback & Growth
I like feedback to be clear and direct, with positive reinforcement, and given in writing — this gives me more time to remember it and act on it.
When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is in writing, clearly and directly, rather than verbally, since important points are easily forgotten that way.
I appreciate it when teammates give me detailed, written instructions and take the time to explain things clearly.
Fun Facts & Quirks
One thing people often misunderstand about me is that I seem quiet — but that usually means I'm thinking and learning from what's being said, not disengaged.
A fun fact about me is that I like to make things as perfect as possible — it's what makes me feel happy and gives me a sense of achievement.
My work superpower is attention to detail and a drive to get things right — I care about making my work as close to perfect as possible, which helps me catch issues others might miss.
