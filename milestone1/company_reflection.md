Company Reflection — Understanding Focus Bear's Mission & Vision
Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

Focus Bear was created by a founder (and later a team) who personally has ADHD and autism. They tried many existing productivity and wellness apps, but found none of them fit how their brains actually work — most mainstream apps assume a "neurotypical" user who can just follow a to-do list or resist distractions with willpower. So they built Focus Bear themselves, specifically to help people like them focus their attention, manage their time, and build routines in a way that works with an ADHD/autistic brain rather than against it.

What problem is Focus Bear solving?

Focus Bear is solving several specific challenges that people with ADHD or autism commonly face. For example, some people experience a mental "blank" when they sit down at their desk and simply can't get started, even when they intend to work. Others struggle with self-regulation — when they hyperfocus on a task, they can ignore basic physiological needs like eating, drinking, or resting for hours. Some people also engage in behaviors like fidgeting, skin-picking, or nail-biting to help themselves concentrate, which can unintentionally cause self-harm. And many struggle with transitioning between tasks — being forced to switch what they're doing can trigger irritability, anxiety, or low mood.

Why do you think this mission is important?

This mission is important because it helps a large and growing number of people — ADHD diagnosis rates have been rising rapidly in recent years — work and study more efficiently instead of constantly fighting against how their brain naturally works. Beyond just productivity, this can genuinely improve their quality of life: less anxiety around unfinished tasks, fewer feelings of failure from "trying and failing" with tools not built for them, and more energy left over for the rest of their life. The fact that Focus Bear has been validated in a randomized controlled trial with real university students also shows this isn't just a nice idea — it has measurable, real-world impact.

How does Focus Bear's work align with your personal values or interests?

Focus Bear's mission of directly helping people resonates with me — I see building software that genuinely improves people's lives as meaningful work, rather than just building technology for its own sake. Knowing that this application can make life brighter for people with ADHD and autism, by helping them work with their brains instead of against them, makes me feel that contributing to this kind of product is a worthwhile use of my skills as a developer.

Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?

To be honest, I often get distracted by small things — a notification, a random thought, or just drifting off task. I'm not diagnosed with ADHD or autism, but I do relate to some of the challenges Focus Bear aims to solve, especially around staying focused and not letting small distractions derail my work. I think Focus Bear's approach — blocking distractions and guiding routines step by step — could genuinely help me eliminate these small interruptions and stay concentrated on my work too.
