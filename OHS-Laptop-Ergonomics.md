# OHS - Laptop Ergonomics

## Research & Learn

**1. What are the risks of using a laptop without an external monitor or keyboard?**
The monitor and keyboard are connected together. If the height of the monitor is appropriate for the neck, it is a little high for the wrist and will damage the wrist. If the height of the keyboard is appropriate for the wrist, it is too low for the neck and will damage the neck.

**2. What ergonomic equipment can improve posture when working on a laptop?**
We can use a laptop stand, external monitor, external keyboard and mouse, and an ergonomic chair to improve posture.

**3. What adjustments should be made to monitor height, chair position, and desk setup for a healthier workspace?**
The external keyboard should be placed at elbow height, with the elbows close to both sides of the body, maintaining a curvature of approximately 90 degrees. The top of the monitor should be just below the line of sight, approximately 20 to 30 inches away from the face. In terms of sitting posture, one should keep feet flat, thighs parallel to the ground, and shoulders relaxed.

**4. What are some daily habits that reduce the impact of prolonged laptop use?**
Get up and move around at regular intervals, check your sitting posture at regular intervals, and do some exercises to relieve eye fatigue (such as the "20-20-20 rule": look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes).
